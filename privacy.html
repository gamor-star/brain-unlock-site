Privacy Policy

BrainUnlock is an iOS parental-control app that helps families build better screen habits. Children complete short thinking challenges before receiving temporary access to apps selected by a parent.

Local-first design

The current version of BrainUnlock is local-first. We do not operate a backend account system, and the app does not intentionally transmit your family's app settings or challenge activity to BrainUnlock servers.

Information stored on your device

BrainUnlock stores the following information locally on the device:

  • Parent access PIN using iOS SecureStore / Keychain-backed storage
  • PIN lockout state used to slow repeated incorrect attempts
  • Parent control settings, including challenge count, unlock duration, categories, and difficulty
  • Temporary unlock session state, including whether access is open and when it expires
  • Local challenge summaries, such as correct answers, unlocks earned, and recent daily totals
  • Apple Screen Time / FamilyControls app-selection token used to apply app shielding

Information we do not collect

The current version of BrainUnlock does not intentionally collect, sell, share, or transmit:

  • Child name
  • Email address or phone number
  • Precise location
  • Contacts
  • Photos or microphone recordings
  • Browsing history or the contents of other apps
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Backend analytics events
  • Payment or subscription records

Screen Time and FamilyControls

BrainUnlock uses Apple's Screen Time and FamilyControls APIs so a parent can choose apps to shield, temporarily remove those shields after successful challenges, and restore shields when the unlock window ends. BrainUnlock does not receive a child's full Screen Time history.

Tracking and advertising

BrainUnlock does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, data broker sharing, or third-party analytics SDKs in the current version.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are not implemented in the current version. If subscriptions, RevenueCat, Apple in-app purchases, backend services, crash reporting, or analytics are added later, this policy will be updated before release.

Reinstalling the app

Some local security data, such as the parent PIN, may survive app deletion and reinstall on the same device because iOS SecureStore uses Keychain-backed storage. Apple Screen Time permission may also remain approved at the system level.
