How it works

Protect. Solve. Open. Relock.

Set it up once. After that, every app you guarded asks for a moment of thinking before it opens — and locks itself back up when your time's up.

  1. 01

    Protect apps

    Pick individual apps or whole categories with Apple's Screen Time picker.

  2. 02

    Solve a challenge

    A short thinking check or puzzle stands between the tap and the app. 30 seconds, not 30 minutes.

  3. 03

    Access opens

    Clear it, and your apps open — for exactly as long as you decided.

  4. 04

    Protection returns

    When your time's up, the shields come back on their own. No willpower required.

Questions before you install

Clear answers, no mystery box.

Screen Time apps ask for trust. These are the details most people want before trying Brain Unlock.

Does Brain Unlock replace Apple Screen Time?

No. Brain Unlock uses Apple Screen Time and FamilyControls APIs to shield selected apps, then temporarily open access after a challenge.

Can Brain Unlock see my full Screen Time history?

No. The app stores the selected app/category token needed to operate shielding. It does not receive a full Screen Time history.

Is this meant to be a game?

No. The challenges are intentionally short. They add useful friction before access, but are not designed as an endless game loop.

What happens when the timer ends?

Protection returns automatically. If iOS behavior varies while an app is already open, shields still apply for relaunch/re-entry.

Does it require an account?

No account is required in the current version. Settings, challenge summaries, and access state are local-first.
