How it works

One calm loop: protect, solve, open, relock.

A predictable rhythm — so setup is obvious and everyday use feels intentional, never random.

  1. 01

    Protect apps

    Pick individual apps or whole categories with Apple's Screen Time picker.

  2. 02

    Solve a challenge

    A short thinking check or puzzle — quick, varied, and worth a moment.

  3. 03

    Access opens

    Protected apps unlock for the window you set in Controls.

  4. 04

    Protection returns

    When the session ends, Brain Unlock restores the shields automatically.

Why it works

A small, mindful pause — then you're in.

Brain Unlock swaps the instant tap-and-scroll for a brief, worthwhile moment of thinking. No streak pressure, no endless game loops — just a calmer way back to the apps you chose to guard.

  • Replaces the reflex. A short challenge interrupts autopilot before a protected app opens.
  • Builds real skills. Recall, logic, reading and problem-solving — woven into everyday unlocking.
  • Access on your terms. Set how many challenges to clear and how long access stays open.
  • No gamification trap. Friction stays purposeful and short — it never becomes the thing you do.
